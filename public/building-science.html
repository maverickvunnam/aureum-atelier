The Four Controls of Building Science

All high-performance homes must manage four critical factors. At Aureum Atelier, we've perfected each of these controls through innovative design and meticulous implementation.

Water Control

Water is the most significant threat to building durability. Our comprehensive water management strategy includes:

  • Advanced drainage planes with redundant water barriers
  • Sophisticated flashing details at all penetrations and transitions
  • Ventilated rain screen systems that promote drainage and drying
  • Strategic material selection for appropriate climate response

These systems work together to direct water away from the building envelope, ensuring long-term protection against moisture damage, mold growth, and structural deterioration.

Advanced flashing details protect against water intrusion
Air Control

Controlling airflow is essential for energy efficiency, comfort, and protecting the building envelope. Our air control strategy includes:

  • Continuous air barrier systems meticulously detailed at all transitions
  • Rigorous air sealing at every penetration and junction
  • Blower door testing at multiple construction phases
  • Balanced ventilation systems for optimal air exchange

Our homes achieve air tightness levels that meet or exceed Passive House standards, resulting in exceptional energy performance and indoor air quality control.

Continuous air barrier installation with verified performance
Vapor Control

Managing water vapor movement is critical to preventing condensation and moisture damage within assemblies. Our vapor control strategy includes:

  • Climate-specific vapor control layer placement and permeance
  • Intelligent material selection based on hygrothermal modeling
  • Strategic temperature gradient management
  • Dynamic vapor control systems that adapt to seasonal changes

This approach prevents condensation within wall and roof assemblies, protecting the structure and insulation from moisture damage and ensuring longevity.

Vapor-intelligent assemblies prevent condensation risk
Thermal Control

Effective thermal control creates comfort, efficiency, and protects building assemblies. Our thermal control strategy includes:

  • Continuous insulation that minimizes thermal bridging
  • Strategic R-value optimization for each assembly
  • High-performance windows and doors with thermal breaks
  • Thermal bridge-free detailing at all transitions

Our thermal control systems not only reduce energy consumption but also contribute to thermal comfort by eliminating cold surfaces and temperature stratification throughout the home.

Building Science FAQ

What does ACH50 mean and why is it important?

ACH50 stands for Air Changes per Hour at 50 Pascals pressure difference, measuring how many times the entire volume of air in a home is replaced in one hour under test conditions. This metric is the industry standard for measuring a home's air tightness.

Lower ACH50 values indicate a tighter building envelope, which means:

  • Less uncontrolled air leakage, resulting in better energy efficiency
  • Improved moisture control within wall assemblies
  • Enhanced comfort with fewer drafts and temperature variations
  • Better sound isolation from outside noise
  • Improved indoor air quality through controlled ventilation

Standard homes typically range from 3-7 ACH50, while our homes achieve 0.6-2.5 ACH50, approaching or meeting Passive House standards.

Won't such a tight house feel stuffy or have air quality issues?

This is a common misconception. In reality, a tightly sealed home with proper mechanical ventilation provides superior air quality compared to leaky homes.

All our homes include balanced ventilation systems (ERV or HRV) that continuously provide filtered fresh air while exhausting stale air. This approach offers several advantages:

  • Filtered fresh air removes pollutants, allergens, and particulates
  • Heat recovery maintains energy efficiency
  • Balanced airflow prevents pressure issues
  • Continuous operation ensures consistent air quality
  • Controlled humidity levels prevent mustiness

Our homes actually have significantly better air quality than conventional homes, where air enters unpredictably through random leaks in the building envelope, bringing in unfiltered outdoor air and potential moisture issues.

How do these building science principles affect maintenance and longevity?

Our building science-driven approach significantly extends the lifespan of your home while reducing maintenance requirements.

Conventional luxury homes often suffer from hidden moisture issues, premature material degradation, and system inefficiencies that lead to costly repairs. Our homes are designed to avoid these problems through:

  • Superior moisture management that prevents mold and rot
  • Proper temperature gradients that prevent condensation
  • Ventilated assemblies that promote drying
  • Reduced thermal stress on materials
  • Protected mechanical systems that operate within optimal ranges

The result is a home that maintains its structural integrity, finish quality, and system performance for decades longer than conventional construction. This translates to lower maintenance costs and preserved property value over time.

Is it worth investing in the premium wall assembly?

The decision between our standard, hybrid, and premium wall assemblies depends on several factors including climate, design requirements, and performance goals.

For many clients, the hybrid assembly represents an optimal balance of performance and value. However, the premium assembly offers significant advantages in certain scenarios:

  • Extreme climate conditions (hot or cold)
  • Homes with architectural features creating significant thermal bridges
  • Projects with the highest performance targets
  • Clients seeking maximum sound isolation
  • Designs with large glazing areas requiring thermal compensation

Our team can provide detailed energy modeling and performance projections to help you evaluate the return on investment for each assembly option based on your specific project requirements.
