Frequently Asked Questions

What information should I prepare for the initial consultation?

To make the most of our initial consultation, we recommend gathering the following information beforehand:

  • Property information (address, survey if available)
  • Images or examples of homes or styles you admire
  • A list of your must-have features and spaces
  • Preliminary budget considerations
  • Your anticipated timeline

Don't worry if you don't have everything prepared—the consultation is designed to help clarify your vision and requirements.

How long does the custom home process typically take?

The timeline for a custom home project varies based on complexity, size, and site conditions, but generally follows this pattern:

  • Design and Planning: 3-6 months
  • Permitting: 1-3 months
  • Construction: 12-18 months

During our consultation process, we'll provide a more detailed timeline specific to your project after understanding your requirements.

Do you work in locations outside of New Jersey?

Yes, we work on select projects throughout the Northeast, including New York, Pennsylvania, and select areas in Massachusetts and Rhode Island. For exceptional projects, we may consider opportunities beyond these areas. During our consultation, we can discuss the logistics and approach for your specific location.

What makes Aureum Atelier different from other luxury builders?

Aureum Atelier distinguishes itself through:

  • Our integrated approach to design, building science, and technology
  • Commitment to both aesthetic excellence and technical performance
  • Limited project volume to ensure exceptional attention to detail
  • Advanced building science methodologies for homes that are as healthy and efficient as they are beautiful
  • Transparent process and communication throughout the project lifecycle

We believe that true luxury lies in the integration of art and science to create homes that perform as beautifully as they look.

Can you work with my architect or designer?

Absolutely. We regularly collaborate with leading architects and designers. Our expertise in building science and technology integration complements their design vision, resulting in homes that are both breathtaking and high-performing. During our consultation, we can discuss the collaborative approach that would work best for your project and existing team.
